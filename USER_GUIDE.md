# Discipline Engine User Guide

## Getting started

Use the bottom dock to switch between Dashboard, Schedule, Pushup Arena, Reels Blocker, PW Study, and Analytics. Camera, Accessibility, and Device Admin controls are only needed when you explicitly activate the related feature.

## Dashboard and RPG progress

- Review your rank, RP total, streak, and equipped title at the top.
- Complete a habit to record its RP reward. Use the alarm card to set or test the daily pushup alarm.
- Open **How to Use & Features** for the in-app walkthrough.

## Schedule

Complete the schedule setup questions, then use the generated daily blocks to organize fixed commitments, P1 study, P2 work, breaks, and rollover tasks. Mark blocks complete as you work.

## Pushup Alarm and camera verification

Arm the alarm when you want the daily reminder. Camera verification starts only from the pushup flow; allow Camera access when Android asks during that action. The verification screen releases camera and pose resources when it closes.

## Reels Blocker

Turn the blocker on from its screen and follow Android's Accessibility settings prompt to enable the service. Configure target apps and the Private DNS guard there. You can disable the service from Android Accessibility settings.

## PW Study AI

- Select **NEET 2.0** or **JEE 2.0** to load a sample syllabus, or paste rows such as `Physics,Electrostatics,8,0,TIER1_HARD_FULL_BATCH` into **Paste / CSV**. Columns are Subject, Chapter, total lectures, completed lectures, and optional tier. Accepted tiers are `TIER1_HARD_FULL_BATCH`, `TIER2_MEDIUM_ONESHOT`, and `TIER3_EASY_NOTES_PYQ`.
- Adjust the exam countdown with the +/- 7 day controls. The offline planner assigns remaining work to dates and recommends Full Batch, YouTube One-Shot, or Notes/PYQ skip strategy based on remaining time and chapter tier.
- Tap **YouTube** beside a One-Shot task to search YouTube for that chapter. Check off a task when complete for +30 RP; completion is saved locally in Room.
- The PW app launch button only opens the installed app. This planner does not request Notification Access and does not scrape PW servers.

## Analytics

Use Analytics to review the heatmap, RPG radar, and activity graphs. Data is recorded locally by the app.
